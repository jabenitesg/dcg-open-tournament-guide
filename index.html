Victoria Open Golf Society

A welcome from our President

Dear valued sponsor:

On behalf of the board of directors of the Victoria Open Golf Society, and all of our volunteers and supporters, I want to thank you for your contribution to making professional golf happen in Victoria.

This is the 45th year since Dave Barr won our first tournament at Glen Meadows in 1981. We are now the oldest tournament on the PGA TOUR Canada, and we have our sponsors and our many volunteers to thank for that achievement.

You all know the tremendous legacy of the Victoria Open, allowing players such as Mike Weir, Steve Stricker, Adam Hadwin, Nick Taylor, McKenzie Hughes, Neil Shipley and many more to get their first taste of professional championship competition. But you may not be aware that your support has also allowed us to make meaningful contributions to charities in our community, with almost a million dollars in the last two years going to Easter Seals for Camp Shawnigan.

Camp Shawnigan provides a vital service to families of children with disabilities on Vancouver Island and around British Columbia. Its current $30 million capital plan will improve its offerings and make sure that children with disabilities and their families will have access to this experience for years to come.

We are very excited to welcome back a previous title sponsor this year with the Digital Commerce Group. Jeff Smith, the founder and CEO, was first involved with our tournament in the 1980s when we were Payless Open, and as one of Victoria’s most prominent success stories, we are proud to associate ourselves with him and his companies. Jeff’s continuing commitment to the community he grew up in is a testament to his values, and the values of his company.

Thanks to Digital Commerce Group and all of our sponsors, we have been able to step up some of our offerings this year. In addition to our VIP hospitality deck for sponsors, and the BBQ tent by the putting green, there is also a new VIP Tent on 19 which offers premium seating, and food and beverage. We are hoping to add more activations on the course in future years to continue to enhance the spectator experience.

As a final note, I want to add a special thanks to the more than 250 volunteers who make this event possible. I also want to recognise our proud partners, the Times Colonist, Andrew Sheret, and Island Eyecare, for providing major support to our tournament.

Finally, I want to thank our host, the Uplands Golf Club, which expends great effort to provide a course in spectacular condition that is truly one of the premier courses in Canada.

Bruce Hallsor

President, Victoria Open Golf Society

PGA TOUR Americas

Welcome to PGA TOUR Americas

PGA TOUR Americas is a showcase of the sport’s best up-and-coming players, and I’m excited for you to be part of our 2026 season and experience what makes this tour great.

This week is part of a 15-event schedule that concludes in October at the PGA Oceans 4 Tour Championship presented by Scotiabank, where the top 10 players on the season-long points list will earn their Korn Ferry Tour cards for 2027.

The first two PGA TOUR Americas season champions—Johnny Keefer in 2024 and Michael Brennan in 2025—have worked their way up to the PGA TOUR this season, and it won’t be long until they are joined by players competing here this week.

Keefer and Brennan are part of a long list of PGA TOUR members who played on our tours, including Nick Taylor, Corey Conners, Adam Hadwin, Jake Knapp, Joel Dahmen, Nico Echavarria and major winner J.J. Spaun.

PGA TOUR Americas has consistently prepared future stars of the PGA TOUR, and I’m confident there are future PGA TOUR winners walking the fairways this week.

Thank you for supporting PGA TOUR Americas, and I hope you enjoy some world-class golf while making great memories with friends and family.

Alex Baldwin

President, Korn Ferry Tour

03 · Before you go

Know before you go

Everything you need for a smooth arrival and a great day at Uplands.

Directions, parking & shuttle

Thanks to our friends at the District of Oak Bay, spectators may use the signed 3-hour parking areas along Cedar Hill Cross Road from the 2300 block to the 2500 block—approximately between Cadboro Bay Road and Henderson Recreation Centre—during tournament play, September 10–13.

Shuttle to Uplands: The first pick-up from Cedar Hill Cross Road is at 7:00 AM Thursday through Sunday, with additional pick-ups every 30 minutes.

Return shuttle: Shuttles depart from the main entrance by the Pro Shop at 7:15 AM, 7:45 AM, 8:15 AM and every 30 minutes thereafter.

Shuttle pick-up: Across from 3600 Cedar Hill Cross Road.

Open shuttle pick-up location ↗
Bags and prohibited items

Opaque bags may not exceed 6 × 6 × 6 inches; clear bags may not exceed 12 × 6 × 12 inches. Leave coolers, large umbrellas and chairs with armrests at home.

Devices and course etiquette

Professional photography cameras are not permitted. Keep phones silent, do not use flash, stay behind gallery ropes, cross only at marked points and follow marshal instructions.

Accessibility, families and gates

Review the official spectator information before travelling for the latest accessibility routes, junior admission guidance and public gate times.
